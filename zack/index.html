The premise

He draws it.
It becomes real.

Zack is a black duck with one golden feather. He holds it in his wing, draws a golden line in mid-air — and the line turns into a real thing, with weight. Only two kinds of things: art and food.

He has no idea why it works. He doesn't make a sound. He isn't trying to be interesting. He just is.

  • One featherIt grows back. Slowly.
  • Art & foodNever animals.
  • Not a wordOnly the sound of what he does.

The cast · six creatures

Everyone wants
something he makes.

They arrive one at a time. Each one finds out what the feather does in an episode of its own.

The Pigeon

The follower

Follows Zack because Zack can draw food. Motionless for ages — then the eyes open, and it's already eaten.

The Seagull

The genius (says he)

Three ducks tall, all chest. Every plan is brilliant. He has never once held the feather.

The Moth

The gold-eater

Eats the golden lines before they become real. No malice, no hurry. Glows a little afterwards.

The Cat

The critic

The only one who sees the artist. Pushes bad art away with one paw. Wide eyes only for the real thing.

The Ant

The inevitable

Never fights, never argues. Walks in at the last second and leaves with the prize.

The Tortoise

The coolest (says he)

Dances. Poses. New luxury sunglasses every single shot. Says only "yeah", "yo", "whoa", "wow".
