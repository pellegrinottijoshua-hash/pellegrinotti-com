The artist · black duck · golden quill

He doesn't know he's an artist.
He knows he's funny.

Zack doesn't talk. He produces exactly one sound — a flat, unimpressed "quac" — and then looks into the camera, so you know it was on purpose.

The golden quill is the whole story: held in his wing, tip forward, it draws glowing gold lines in mid-air — and whatever he draws becomes real. A door that opens the wrong way. A ladder missing exactly one rung. An umbrella with a hole precisely above his head. Art finds a way. Usually the wrong one.

The quill
Gold, held in the wing, draws with the tip. In this world, all ink is black — the gold is his alone.
The wrong detail
Every creation works, except for one small thing. The one small thing is the episode.
The Crollo
Roughly once in ten disasters he collapses into explosive laughter. Then hard cut — deadpan again.
The supporting cast · one flaw each

Everyone wants something he makes.

Five creatures orbit the Atelier, each after a different piece of Zack's talent: the food, the gold, the art, the quill — or whatever is left unattended.
