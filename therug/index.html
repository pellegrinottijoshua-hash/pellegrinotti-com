File 01 · The hero

He could save the world. He'd rather not.

The Rug is 45-ish, built like a former athlete who discovered couches, and wears a cape that is — legally and spiritually — a genuine persian carpet. Bordeaux, ochre, ivory. Frayed fringe. It hangs with the true weight of real carpet, because it is one.

When giant alien warriors — a proud race whose single vanity is the immaculately groomed moustache — descend on his city, he does what any of us would do: he sighs, mutters "Uh… fine," and shuffles out in his Birkenstocks.
